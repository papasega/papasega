## About Me

I am an AI Research Engineer at Orange Innovation with a PhD in Computer Science from IMT Atlantique.

My work focuses on large language models, speech technologies, and AI systems for African languages, with a strong interest in digital inclusion and real-world impact. I am especially interested in building tools and research that help languages like Wolof thrive in the AI era.

---

## What I Work On

- Large Language Models for low-resource and multilingual settings
- Speech technologies, including ASR and TTS
- Natural Language Processing for African languages
- Wolof language technologies
- Applied deep learning for inclusive AI
